Welcome to my blog. I created this space to document what I learn, the projects I build, and my reflections on web development and artificial intelligence.

## Why this blog?

Throughout my career, I have accumulated knowledge and practical experience that I want to share with the wider community. Here I will explain complex ideas clearly, discuss technical challenges, document discoveries, and contribute useful resources for developers and researchers.

## Topics I will explore

The articles will cover modern web development with React, Next.js, and TypeScript, alongside machine learning, deep learning, computer vision, virtual reality, and cognitive science.

This website is itself an evolving laboratory: a multilingual academic platform built with Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and TinaCMS.

## Stay connected

You are welcome to contact me with questions, ideas, or collaboration proposals. Sharing knowledge remains one of the most effective ways to deepen our own understanding.
